# Student Handbook - PGDM Programme (Sample)

## Fee Payment Deadlines
Term fees must be paid on or before the 10th day of the first month of each term. A late fee of INR 500 per day is charged after the deadline, up to a maximum of INR 5,000.

## Payment Portal Failure
If the online payment portal fails due to a technical error on the institute side, the student may pay on the next working day without any late fee. The student must keep a screenshot of the error and raise a support request on the same day. Failures caused by the student's bank are not covered by this rule.

## Fee Refund Policy
Students who withdraw within 15 days of the start of the term receive a 90 percent refund of tuition fees. No refund is given after 30 days from the start of the term. Hostel fees are non-refundable.

## Merit Scholarship
Merit scholarships are awarded to students with a CGPA of 3.5 or above at the end of each term. The scholarship covers 25 percent of the tuition fee for the next term. Scholarship status is reviewed every term by the scholarship committee.

## Need-Based Scholarship
Need-based scholarships are available to students with annual family income below INR 6 lakh. Applications must be submitted within the first 20 days of the term with income proof. Results are announced within 30 days.

## Course Change (Elective Change)
A student may change an elective course within the first 14 days of the term. Every course change request must be approved by the Programme Office. Only one course change is allowed per term.

## Attendance Requirement
Students must maintain at least 75 percent attendance in every course. Students below 75 percent are not allowed to appear for the end-term examination of that course.

## Support Requests
Students can raise a support request for IT, fees, academics or hostel issues. Support requests are normally answered within 2 working days. Urgent IT issues are answered within 4 working hours.

## Data Privacy
Student records are confidential. A student may view only their own records. Staff must never share passwords or OTPs, and students must never share an OTP or password with anyone, including the support desk.
